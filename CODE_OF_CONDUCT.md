# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We commit to providing a welcoming, inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of background.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive in all communication.
- Welcome feedback and different perspectives.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward others.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech.
- Deliberate intimidation or threats.
- Spam or commercial solicitation.

## Enforcement

Violations are taken seriously. Offenders may be banned from the project.

Report misconduct to the maintainers.
